Privacy Policy

Application: my-tiktok-uploader · Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. Summary

This application is a personal tool used by its author to upload the author's own videos to TikTok. It does not collect, sell, or share personal data with anyone. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

2. What data is handled

DataWhyWhere it is stored
OAuth access token and refresh token To authorise upload requests to TikTok on the author's behalf Locally, in a file on the author's own computer — never sent anywhere else
Basic TikTok profile information returned by the API (such as open ID and display name) To confirm which account is connected Locally, on the author's own computer
Video files chosen for upload To publish them to TikTok at the author's request On the author's own computer; transferred only to TikTok during upload

3. What data is NOT collected

  • No personal data of any other person is collected or processed.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry is used.
  • No cookies are set by this application.
  • Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

4. Third parties

The only third party involved is TikTok itself, because videos are transmitted to TikTok's servers via its official Content Posting API. TikTok's own privacy policy governs how TikTok handles your data after upload.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens and profile data stay on the author's computer until the author deletes them, which can be done at any time by deleting the local credentials file. You can also revoke the application's access at any moment from your TikTok account settings: Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.

6. Security

Credentials are kept locally and are not transmitted to any server other than TikTok's own API endpoints over HTTPS. The Client Secret is stored only on the author's machine.

7. Children

The application is not intended for, and is not made available to, children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's behaviour changes. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

For any privacy question, write to the address listed in the application's developer profile on TikTok for Developers.
